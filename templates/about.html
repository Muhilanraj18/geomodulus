About GeoModulus AI

AI-based soil elastic modulus prediction and engineering decision-support system.

Project Overview

GeoModulus AI is a machine-learning-based system designed to predict soil elastic modulus values from laboratory and field soil parameters.

The system analyzes soil characteristics such as Liquid Limit, Plastic Limit, Plasticity Index, Optimum Moisture Content, Maximum Dry Density, CBR, reinforcement condition and soaking condition.

The predicted modulus values are then used to generate an engineering-oriented assessment for decision support.
